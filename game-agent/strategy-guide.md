# Corbie's Princess Maker 2 Refine field guide

Goal: finish alive with any adulthood ending. This is a reference, not a fixed walkthrough. Current game evidence takes priority. Community advice can differ between editions; verify names, costs and availability in Refine.

## Choosing jobs

Farm builds stamina and strength, pays comparatively well early, but adds stress and reduces elegance. It is useful for early conditioning, not an automatic lifetime career.

Inn and Restaurant offer income with less daily stress than Farm. Inn develops cleaning; Restaurant develops cooking. Both can reduce combat skill. Church pays little but develops faith and morality, reduces sin, and adds less stress. Babysitting develops sensitivity but pays less and adds substantial stress. Housework develops domestic skills without regular wages.

Source for these tradeoffs: [Refine community guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=771709910), Part Time Jobs. Summarized rather than copied; displayed job descriptions take priority.

## Health and recovery

Compare stress with stamina/constitution, not age or measurements. Weak constitution makes work harder and illness more likely. Early farming can build constitution; it should not replace recovery. Church can support faith and morality. Free time and sick-bed rest are different screens; read the offered choices before acting.

## Corbie's planning policy

Before each monthly schedule, check health, seasonal clothing, daughter conversation and the town/castle opportunity. Use the current game month and the existing social record.

For each period, weigh recovery, money and development. Do not mechanically fill two work periods or always pick Farm. Reconsider the same job if recent choices already favored it. Repetition is allowed when current evidence justifies it; variety alone is not a strategy. Include recovery before stress becomes dangerous.

If money is low, compare currently offered paid jobs and their demands. If stamina is visibly weak and stress manageable, Farm may help. If stress is concerning, consider rest or a less stressful available job. If stable and solvent, consider useful development rather than more physical work. Preserve money for living costs and clothing. Study needs a visible cost and enough money after the expense; otherwise inspect or pause. Do not assume an older-age job is unlocked.

Read current stats from the picture or explicitly labelled text. Unknown values stay unknown. If a missing stat or cost is necessary to choose responsibly, inspect through a recognized control or pause. Do not fabricate wages, stamina, successful work days or stat gains. Explain the choice using a current observation and a relevant tradeoff.
