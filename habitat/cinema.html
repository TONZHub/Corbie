Before recording

Open this from Corbie v0.100 → Nest → Behavior → Open cinema. Use an awake, unpaused raven. Set walls to keep wandering and flights inside the recording frame. Close the old habitat tab so the two pages do not compete for his attention. Keep the browser in a normal window; F11 or presentation mode can make him rest.

Choose your frame and Set walls. Drag a box around the cinema frame when the overlay appears, then place Corbie on the branch. Moving or resizing the browser, changing the layout, or showing the controls opens the walls; press B to set them again. Click the forest, then press C to rewind and start a three-second count-in. Crop your display recording to the stage. There is no sound, so you can add your own soundtrack later.

The visible canvas is sampled into his motion senses. Reactions depend on his mood and familiarity; these are not scripted animations or spoken lines. Titles and decorations are outside his pixel feed. Opening the HTML directly plays the film, but live reactions need the Nest connection.
